Privacy Policy for The Big Stick War Mobile

Old Deer Games · Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play The Big Stick War Mobile (package com.OldDeerGames.TheBigStickWarMobile), why it is collected, and what choices you have. The game is developed and published by Old Deer Games.

Summary

  • You can play the whole game without an account. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.
  • An account is optional and only needed for the Weekly Challenge and its online leaderboard.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob, which uses some device information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the game or through our account deletion page.

Information collected when you play without an account

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details. Your game progress, settings and records are stored only on your device and are removed if you uninstall the game.

The game shows advertising, and advertising requires some information about your device to work. When an ad is requested, the following may be collected and processed by our advertising partner:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it, such as country or city
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version
  • Information about the ads shown to you, such as which ads were displayed, viewed or clicked

This information is used to deliver ads, to measure whether they worked, and to detect fraudulent or invalid ad activity.

Information collected if you create an account

To take part in the Weekly Challenge you can sign in with a Unity Player Account. If you do, the following information is processed:

  • Sign-in details. You enter your email address and password on Unity's own sign-in page. Unity handles them; we never see or store your password. Our game receives a player identifier that represents your account.
  • Nickname. The game nickname you choose. It is public: other players can see it on the Weekly Challenge player list and leaderboards.
  • Weekly Challenge data. Which challenges you registered for, your scores, and a short summary of each attempt (for example wave times, enemies defeated and units bought). The summaries are used to calculate scores and to check that results are fair.

We use this information only to run your account, the Weekly Challenge and its leaderboards, to keep the competition fair, and to answer your requests.

Who processes this information

Advertising — Google

Advertising in the game is provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google acts as an independent data controller for the advertising data it collects and may share it with its advertising partners. How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Accounts and online features — Unity

Accounts, the player list, leaderboards and challenge data are provided by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Unity Player Accounts, Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code and Leaderboards. This data is stored on Unity's servers. Unity's handling of player data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

We do not share your information with anyone other than the service providers described above, except where required by law.

Personalised advertising and your consent

Ads may be personalised, meaning they are selected based on information about your device and your previous activity, or non-personalised, meaning they are selected based only on general context such as your approximate location.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used. This is done through Google's User Messaging Platform, which appears when you first open the game. You may accept or refuse, and refusing does not prevent you from playing — you will simply see non-personalised ads instead.

You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's settings menu.

Controlling advertising on your device

Android lets you limit how your advertising identifier is used. Open Settings → Google → Ads on your device, where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops apps from using it to personalise advertising.

How long data is kept

  • Game progress without an account stays on your device until you uninstall the game.
  • Account data (nickname, registrations, scores and attempt summaries) is kept while your account exists, so that leaderboards and past results stay correct. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the game: open the Account panel (profile button on the main menu) and choose Delete account.
  • Without the game: follow the instructions on our account deletion page, or email us at olddeergames@gmail.com.

Deleting your account removes your nickname, registrations, scores and attempt summaries from our game. Your Unity Player Account itself can also be used with other games; to delete it completely, use Unity's Unity Player Account deletion page.

Children

The Big Stick War Mobile is rated 16+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent you previously gave. You can contact us at the address below and we will help. For advertising data you can also contact Google, and for your Unity Player Account you can also contact Unity, using the details in their privacy policies.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes or as legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Continuing to use the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to olddeergames@gmail.com.
