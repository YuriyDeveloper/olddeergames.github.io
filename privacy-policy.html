Privacy Policy for The Big Stick War Mobile

Old Deer Games · Effective 16 September 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play The Big Stick War Mobile (package com.OldDeerGames.TheBigStickWarMobile), why it is collected, and what choices you have. The game is developed and published by Old Deer Games.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

The game shows advertising, and advertising requires some information about your device to work. When an ad is requested, the following may be collected and processed by our advertising partner:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it, such as country or city
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version
  • Information about the ads shown to you, such as which ads were displayed, viewed, or clicked

This information is used to deliver ads, to measure whether they worked, and to detect fraudulent or invalid ad activity.

Who processes this information

Advertising in the game is provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google acts as an independent data controller for the advertising data it collects and may share it with its advertising partners.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising provider described above.

Personalised advertising and your consent

Ads may be personalised, meaning they are selected based on information about your device and your previous activity, or non-personalised, meaning they are selected based only on general context such as your approximate location.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used. This is done through Google's User Messaging Platform, which appears when you first open the game. You may accept or refuse, and refusing does not prevent you from playing — you will simply see non-personalised ads instead.

You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's settings menu.

Controlling advertising on your device

Regardless of where you live, Android lets you limit how your advertising identifier is used. Open Settings → Google → Ads on your device, where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops apps from using it to personalise advertising.

Children

The Big Stick War Mobile is rated 16+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

How long data is kept

We do not operate servers and do not store your data ourselves. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies, described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent you previously gave. To exercise rights relating to advertising data, contact Google using the details in its privacy policy. You can also contact us at the address below and we will help where we are able to.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes or as legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Continuing to use the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to olddeergames@gmail.com.
