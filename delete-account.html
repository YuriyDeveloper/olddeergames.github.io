Delete Your The Big Stick War Mobile Account

Old Deer Games

You can delete your The Big Stick War Mobile account and its data at any time, in the game or without it.

Option 1: in the game

  1. Open The Big Stick War Mobile.
  2. On the main menu, tap the profile button to open the Account panel.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: without the game

Send an email to olddeergames@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Include:

  • your game nickname, and
  • the email address of your Unity Player Account.

Please send the request from that same email address, so we can confirm the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your nickname
  • Your Weekly Challenge registrations
  • Your scores and leaderboard entries
  • Your attempt summaries (wave times, enemies defeated, units bought)
  • Your player account in our game

Game progress stored on your device without an account is not affected; it is removed when you uninstall the game.

Your Unity Player Account

You sign in with a Unity Player Account, which can also be used with other games. Deleting your account in our game removes your data from our game. To delete the Unity Player Account itself, use Unity's Unity Player Account deletion page.

Advertising data

Advertising data is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID on your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads. More details are in our Privacy Policy.
